An interactive 6-page Power BI dashboard analysing the 2024 Indian general election results across alliances, states and constituencies.

## Pages
- **Landing Page:** navigation hub
- **Overview Analysis:** alliance-wise seat share (NDA, I.N.D.I.A., Others) with party-wise counts and percentages
- **State Demographics Analysis:** state-level demographic view
- **Political Landscape by State:** party and alliance strength on a filled map
- **Constituency Analysis:** drill-through to individual constituency results
- **Details Grid:** detailed table of results

## What I built
- Filled maps, donut charts and a party-wise pivot table
- Data model and DAX measures for victory margin, vote share and winning alliance per seat
- Page navigation buttons and drill-through across constituencies

## Tech
Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Data Modelling

## Data
Publicly available 2024 Lok Sabha election results.

## How to open
Download `Election 2024.pbix` and open it in Power BI Desktop (free).
